Frequently asked questions

Is Visa Radar an official government site?

No. Visa Radar is an independent information service and is not affiliated with any government, embassy or airline. Always confirm entry rules on the official source.

How often is the data updated?

The site rebuilds from the latest open dataset every week. The current snapshot is dated 17 February 2026, and every page footer shows it.

Why might my result differ from the embassy?

Our data is a community-maintained snapshot and rules can change at short notice. The embassy or official portal always takes priority.

What is the difference between an eTA and a visa?

An eTA is a light online authorisation linked to your passport, while a visa is a fuller permission that usually needs more documents and time.

Do I need a visa just to change planes?

Sometimes. It depends on your passport, the airport and whether you leave the transit area. Check the official site and your airline.

How much passport validity do I need?

It depends on the destination. Many ask for three or six months beyond your stay. Use our passport validity checker once you know your destination's rule.

Can I apply for ETIAS now?

No. ETIAS has not opened, so any site offering an application is not legitimate. See our ETIAS page for the latest status.

How do I report a mistake?

Use the report link on any route page or email us with a link to the official source.
