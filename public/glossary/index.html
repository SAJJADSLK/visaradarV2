Glossary

90/180 rule
Limit of 90 days in any rolling 180-day period for visa-exempt visitors to the Schengen Area.
Airside transit
Changing planes without leaving the international area or passing border control.
Biometrics
Fingerprints and a facial image collected at an embassy, visa centre or border.
eTA
Electronic travel authorisation: an online pre-check linked to your passport, not a full visa.
e-Visa
A visa applied for and approved online before travel.
EES
The EU Entry/Exit System, a digital record of entries and exits for short-stay non-EU visitors.
ETIAS
A planned EU travel authorisation for visa-exempt visitors. Not open for applications.
Landside transit
Entering a country during a layover, which usually needs entry permission.
Overstay
Staying beyond the permitted period, which can bring fines, bans and removal.
Schengen Area
A group of European countries without internal border checks that share a common short-stay visa policy.
Travel freedom score
On Visa Radar, the number of destinations a passport reaches visa-free, on arrival or with an eTA.
Validity rule
A destination's minimum remaining passport validity, such as three or six months.
Visa on arrival
A visa issued at the border or airport, usually for a fee.
Visa-free
Entry without a prior visa application, for a set number of days.
