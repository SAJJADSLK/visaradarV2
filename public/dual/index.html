Dual passport planner

Hold two or three passports? See which one gives the easiest entry to each destination, and how many places they reach together.

Many countries require you to enter and leave on the passport matching your nationality there, and some treat dual nationals as citizens only. Airlines also check that your passport matches your booking. Confirm the rules for each country before you choose.
