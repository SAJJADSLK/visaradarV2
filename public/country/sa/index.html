Before you go to Saudi Arabia

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months, with blank pages.
  • Travel insurance covering medical care and cancellation.
  • Notify your bank, and carry some SAR cash.
  • Check that your devices fit G plugs, and save the +966 calling code for emergencies.
  • Drive on the right side if renting a car, and check whether you need an international driving permit.
