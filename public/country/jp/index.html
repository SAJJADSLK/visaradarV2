Before you go to Japan

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months, with blank pages.
  • Travel insurance covering medical care and cancellation.
  • Notify your bank, and carry some JPY cash.
  • Check that your devices fit A plugs, and save the +81 calling code for emergencies.
  • Drive on the left side if renting a car, and check whether you need an international driving permit.
