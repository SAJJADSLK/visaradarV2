ETIAS travel authorisation

ETIAS will be an online authorisation for visa-exempt travellers to Schengen countries. It is not open yet.

Status reviewed 08 October 2026 from public reporting. Rules and dates change, so check the official portals: EES and ETIAS.

Status

Earlier EU communication pointed to the last quarter of 2026. Newer reporting says the official portal dropped that window and a launch in 2027 looks likely, with no confirmed date. We will update this page when the EU announces one.

Key facts reported so far

  • Fee: EUR 20, valid for three years, after an increase from the original EUR 7.
  • Travellers under 18 and over 70 are reported to be exempt from the fee but still need to register.
  • Applications are made on the official EU site only. Anyone selling ETIAS applications now is not legitimate.
