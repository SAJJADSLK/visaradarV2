The Schengen 90/180-day rule explained

How the rolling 180-day window works and how to stay within it.

The rule

Visa-exempt visitors may stay in the Schengen Area for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. The 180 days is a rolling window that looks back from each day, not a calendar half-year.

One area, one limit

Days in all Schengen countries are added together. Countries outside Schengen, such as the UK, do not count. Check the current member list before you plan.

Counting days

  • Both the day you enter and the day you leave count.
  • Short trips add up. Two 45-day visits within 180 days use the whole allowance.
  • Since the Entry/Exit System, border systems record your days automatically.
