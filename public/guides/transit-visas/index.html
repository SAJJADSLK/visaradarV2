Do you need a visa to change planes?

Airside and landside transit, and what to check before booking a connection.

Airside transit

You stay inside the international area and never pass border control. Many nationalities need no visa for this, but some do, for specific airports.

Landside transit

You enter the country, for example to collect bags on separate tickets or switch airports. This often needs the same entry permission as a visit.

What to check

  • Whether your tickets are on one booking, so bags are checked through.
  • The transit rules for your passport at each connecting country.
  • Your airline's guidance, since they can refuse boarding.

Our data

Visa Radar's dataset covers entry rules, not transit rules. Always confirm transit on the official site or with your airline.
