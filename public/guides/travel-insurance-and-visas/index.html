Travel insurance and visas: what to know

When insurance is required and what to look for in a policy.

Where it is required

Some visa types and destinations ask for proof of insurance, and applicants for a Schengen visa must show medical cover that meets the minimum rules. Check the requirement for your category.

Even where it is optional

Medical treatment, evacuation and repatriation abroad can be very expensive. Insurance is worth having for almost any international trip.

What to look for

  • Medical cover with emergency evacuation and repatriation.
  • Trip cancellation and baggage cover.
  • Clear terms on pre-existing conditions and activities such as hiking or driving.

Keep proof handy

Carry the policy certificate on paper and on your phone, ideally with an English version.
