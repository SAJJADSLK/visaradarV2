Passport renewal checklist: when and how to renew

Renew early enough that validity rules never catch you out.

When to renew

Many destinations ask for three or six months of remaining validity, so a passport that looks fine can fail the rule. Test your dates with our validity checker and renew well before you book.

What you will usually need

  • Your current passport and the application form from your issuing authority.
  • Photos that meet the official specification.
  • The fee and any identity or citizenship documents the authority requires.

Timing

Issuing authorities publish processing times and they change with demand. Apply early and avoid buying non-refundable travel until the new passport is in your hands.

After you receive it

Check every detail and sign it if required. Authorisations linked to your old passport number, such as an eTA, normally have to be applied for again.
