How to avoid visa and eTA scams

Lookalike sites charge extra for free or low-cost official services. Here is how to spot them.

Check the address

Official immigration sites normally sit on a government domain. Be wary of sites that rank high in search ads but use a generic .com, .org or .info address.

Know what is free

Some official systems cost little or nothing, and imitators add large service fees for the same form. Compare the price with the official portal linked on each Visa Radar destination page.

Systems that are not open yet

Some schemes have been announced but cannot be applied for. Any site selling an application for a scheme that has not launched, such as ETIAS at the time of writing, should be treated as a scam. See our ETIAS page.

Protect your documents

  • Do not send a passport scan to a site you have not verified.
  • Pay only on a secure page and keep the receipt.
  • If you paid a fake site, contact your bank or card issuer straight away and report it to your local consumer authority.
