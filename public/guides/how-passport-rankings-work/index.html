How passport rankings work and why sites disagree

What a travel freedom score measures, and the limits of any ranking.

The score

On Visa Radar, a passport's score is the number of destinations it reaches visa-free, on arrival or with an electronic authorisation. At our 17 February 2026 snapshot the leaders were United Arab Emirates (169), Singapore (163), South Korea (163). See the full global rank.

Why numbers differ

Rankings depend on the data source, the date and what counts as access. Some indexes use airline data and some use community-maintained data. Some count e-visas and others do not. Two honest rankings can therefore differ.

What a ranking cannot tell you

A score says nothing about how easy a visa is to get, how long you can stay or whether rules for your trip changed last week. Check the changes tracker and the official source before you travel.
