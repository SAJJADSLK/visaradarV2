Tourist, business, work, study or transit: which category fits?

Why the purpose of your trip decides the permission you need.

Tourism and visiting family

Short leisure trips and visits to family or friends usually fall under visitor or tourist rules. Paid work is not allowed.

Business visits

Meetings, conferences and contract talks are often allowed on a visitor permission, but selling directly to the public or doing paid work usually is not. The wording of the rules differs, so check what counts as business activity.

Work and study

Employment and courses of study almost always need a specific visa, even where visitors are visa-free. Entering as a tourist and then working or studying can lead to refusal, removal and entry bans.

Transit

Passing through a country on the way to a third destination has its own rules. Read our transit guide.
