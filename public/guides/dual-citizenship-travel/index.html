Travelling with two passports: the basics

How to use dual nationality sensibly when you travel.

Use the right passport at the border

Many countries expect their own citizens to enter and leave on their own passport, and some treat dual nationals as citizens only. Check the rules for each country you will pass through.

Keep your documents consistent

The passport you give the airline should match your booking and any visa or eTA. Switching passports mid-trip can cause check-in problems.
