How to plan your visa timeline

Work backwards from your departure date and add buffers.

Work backwards

Start from the date you must have the visa. Subtract processing time, the wait for an appointment, and time for your passport to be returned if it is held.

Add buffers

Processing times can change without notice, and peak seasons slow everything down. A buffer of several weeks is safer than a tight schedule.

Book smart

Where you can, choose flexible or refundable tickets until the visa is granted. Check the official source for current timings.
