Passport validity checker

Airlines can refuse boarding if your passport is too close to expiry. Pick the rule your destination states, enter your dates, and check.

The common rules

  • Six-month rule: some countries want at least six months of validity, counted from your arrival or your departure. The destination decides which.
  • Three-month rule: the Schengen Area requires validity of at least three months after you plan to leave, and the passport must have been issued within the previous 10 years.
  • Length of stay: some countries only need the passport to be valid for the whole stay.
  • Blank pages: some countries also require one or two blank pages.

Rules differ by country and change. We do not publish a country-by-country list because we could not verify one from an authoritative source. Find your destination's rule on its official site (linked on each destination page), then pick it above.
