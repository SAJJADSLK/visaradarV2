Apply online for an electronic travel authorisation before you fly. Airlines check it at check-in.

Confirm this on the official Australia government or embassy site. Entry rules can change with little notice.

Your checklist

  1. Apply on the official government site only. Lookalike sites charge extra.
  2. Apply well ahead of departure and keep the approval email.
  3. Use the same passport to travel that you applied with.
  4. Check the fee and validity period on the official site.
