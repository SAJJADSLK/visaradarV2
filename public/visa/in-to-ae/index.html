Your checklist

  1. Find the embassy, consulate or official e-visa portal.
  2. Gather passport, photos, bank statements, itinerary and insurance.
  3. Book biometrics or interview appointments early.
  4. Apply before booking non-refundable travel.
  5. Track the application and allow buffer time.
