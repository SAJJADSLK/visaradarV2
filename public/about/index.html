About Visa Radar

Visa Radar publishes passport-to-destination entry rules and country travel guides. Every rule page shows when it was last reviewed, and pages older than 180 days carry a warning.

How we work

  • Rules are stored in one dataset and rebuilt into static pages.
  • Corrections are logged and dated.
  • We are not a government agency or a visa service, and nothing here is legal advice.

Spot an error? Tell us and include an official source link.
